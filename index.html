Pre-Order & Reheating FAQ

When is the pre-order cut-off time?

Pre-orders close at 8:00 PM the night before each drop day (or earlier if that day's batch sells out!). Book early to lock in your order.

How does payment work?

After filling in your details, tap Continue to Payment and choose how to pay. DuitNow QR / Transfer / Touch 'n Go: scan our QR (Maybank or Touch 'n Go, your choice) or use the bank details, then upload a screenshot of your receipt — your slot is reserved once we verify the payment. Cash: no receipt needed, just pay in cash when you collect or when your order is delivered.

How much is delivery?

Desa Puteri is free. BTP is RM 2. GV is RM 3.Batu Arang, Kuang, Kota Puteri and Kundang are RM 4. The fee is added to your total at checkout.

How do I store and reheat leftovers?

All pans are packed in oven- and air-fryer-safe aluminum trays. Cover with foil and reheat at 170°C for 12–15 minutes (remove foil in the last 3 minutes for a bubbling cheese crust), or microwave for 90 seconds. Keeps chilled up to 3 days.
